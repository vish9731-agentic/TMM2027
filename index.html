3-Day Strength & Injury Armor System

Targeted specifically to eradicate calf cramps at km 18–19 and eliminate lower back fatigue.

Tuesday (35 mins) Lower Body

Calf Armor & Achilles

Builds eccentric muscle resilience so calves do not fail under marathon load.

  • 1. Single-Leg Eccentric Heel Drops 3 x 15 / leg
    Lower down slowly over 3 full seconds on a stair step. Directly targets gastrocnemius & Achilles stiffness.
  • 2. Seated Bent-Knee Calf Raises 3 x 15 reps
    Place weight or backpack on knees. Isolates the soleus muscle (primary shock absorber during long runs).
  • 3. Tibialis Anterior Wall Raises 3 x 20 reps
    Lean back against a wall, lift toes high. Eliminates shin splints and anterior muscle fatigue.
  • 4. Bulgarian Split Squats 3 x 8-10 / leg
    Strengthens quads and knee stabilizers without compressing the lower back.
  • 5. Single-Leg Glute Bridges 3 x 12 / leg
    Hold top position for 2 seconds. Fires gluteus medius/maximus to take load off the lumbar spine.
Thursday (30 mins) Core & Spine

Posterior Chain & Lumbar Core

Stops lower back aching and supports upright posture when fatigue sets in.

  • 1. Romanian Deadlifts (RDLs) 3 x 10 reps
    Hinge hips back with flat spine. Strengthens hamstrings and lumbar erector muscles.
  • 2. Bird-Dogs (with 3-sec pause) 3 x 10 / side
    Deep transverse abdominis & multifidus stability. Prevents lumbar spine extension faults.
  • 3. Side Planks (Pelvic Lock) 3 x 35-45s / side
    Strengthens quadratus lumborum & obliques to stop side-to-side hip drop when running.
  • 4. Deadbugs 3 x 12 alternating
    Anti-extension core drill. Teaches your rib cage to stay locked over your pelvis.
  • 5. Supermans / Prone Cobras 3 x 12 reps
    Endurance for upper and lower back muscles.
Monday Post-Run (15 mins) Mobility

Hip & Calf Tissue Flush

Quick recovery routine immediately following Monday's short shakeout run.

  • 1. Foam Roll Calves & Quads 30s / muscle
    Gentle sweeping passes over tight knots. Do not aggressively mash inflamed tendons.
  • 2. Couch Stretch / Hip Flexor Stretch 60s / side
    Unlocks tight psoas muscles that pull directly on your lower back.
  • 3. Pigeon Pose / Figure-4 60s / side
    Releases deep piriformis and gluteal tension.
  • 4. Cat-Cow Segmental Mobility 10 slow breaths
    Restores gentle articulation throughout the entire spine.

Anti-Cramp Hydration & Fueling Protocol

Engineered to overcome sodium depletion in Mumbai's coastal humidity ($65\% - 85\%$).

⚡ Energy Gels
1 Gel every 40–45 minutes ($30 - 45\text{g}$ carbs/hr). Always wash down with $150\text{ ml}$ plain water.
🧂 Salt Capsules
1 Capsule ($200\text{mg}$ sodium) every 45–60 mins. Take at km 15–16 before the cramp threshold!
💧 Fluid Timing
$150 - 200\text{ ml}$ small sips every 15–20 mins. Do not gulp large volumes all at once.
